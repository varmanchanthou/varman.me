My Complete Life Story & Time Capsule (2011–2026)

Hello, My name is Varman [Full name Varman Chanthou / Chanthou Varman (Khmer official name: ចន្ធូ វរ្ម័ន)]. I am just a normal person living in Siem Reap city, Cambodia (a beautiful place I love to live and spend my time). I was born on 9th Sep 2011 in Wat Bo, Siem Reap city, Cambodia. I was born to a normal family of Chanthou Choun or Chanthou Churn (my dad). My mom told me that when I was born, there was a heavy flood that flooded the city for a whole week (9th Sep 2011). I was raised by my parents and I was spoiled.

At the time, because my mom and dad were very busy, they sent me to USA International School 1 (USAIS). It is fun because I remember that someone bit me and the next day my dad told me that “if someone bites u, u can bite him back” — so I bit someone the next day and then they called my dad! I had so many memories at USAIS. I started studying Khmer at the age of 4 (kindergarten) at Angkor Kindergarten until 2015. I started Grade 1 at the age of 5. I remember I didn’t like school at first. I always went late and I hated school, but because of my teacher Vearn Marina, I started adapting to the school. The first month of school I got number 14, then number 7, then number 1; because of her, I started to study hard and I liked school (class of 2016–2017), and I started to lock in with school. NGL, G1 was the best time ever; I had so many friends and fair, good competitors. I got number 2 in the First grade. Grade 2 was normal, I kinda enjoyed it but not a lot. I started to study Chinese in Grade 2. Life was great (from Grade 1–5). Grade 5 was also one of the best years; I had good friends and had a lot of experiences.

From 2019 to 2022 when there was COVID, I started to realize about life; I started learning about technologies by myself and tried to know about it. I started to know about computers when I played games on them. It was so okay. In those years I also got to know a lot about money (Finance) too.

My dad used to tell me that “At first, after graduating high school, he had only $50 in his pocket; but because he tried and never gave up at the time, he managed to be an owner of one of the boutiques in town (Horizon Cambodia).” Because of that, I got to meet a lot of foreigners and people all around the world and get to know everyone. They gave me experiences and motivation. So because of my father’s ambition (from $50), I can’t be worse than my father — only better than him 😊. I started to learn about money (Finance), technologies, and psychology (a bit) from Grade 4 on. At the end of Grade 5, I remember there was a competition at school by the ministry SeangNam and I won. I went into Grade 7 at the age of 10 and it was great. I doubted myself that I would be bad and get a bad grade because I skipped Grade 6. At first, I was like, I don’t think I would beat them or something, but then I beat them. In Grade 8 my class was full of smart students because the school gathered them in one class. Somehow, I managed to beat them too. In Grade 8, I was kinda bad at first but I tried to study pretty hard and my technique to study was to memorize all the lessons — though now I change to another technique. Grade 9 was fun even if it felt like wasting time. I did really good in Physics at that time because I got top 1 in my school in the Physics sector.

After Grade 9, I went into Grade 10 which is in 2026 (now). It’s one of the most depressed and one of the most relaxed years of my life. I tried and faced so many things. First of all, I learned a lot more about life; I started to learn a lot from books and I started to do things I like. Because I tried to change my class from Maths class to Physics class and they didn’t accept and I didn’t like the environment, I completely changed my mind and started to reconnect with classmates in class and started to talk to all of them even when it wasn’t going well. Right now I have a few friends and associates which is more than enough — but if you guys reading this want to connect with me, I’m happy to connect to you! There was actually a border dispute between Cambodia and Thailand; so my father and I were the ones who organized the march and protest in Siem Reap to protest and call for the release of the 18 soldiers (two times), and we also worked with Mr. Tan Kimsour to make the Art for Peace too. I got to meet a lot of big people and more.

Right now, I am in trading, coding, technologies, and finance. I’ve been in trading since I was in Grade 7 and tried to learn about it more and more. Here’s my Telegram trading signals: t.me/meowtrading2026. I also do some Shopify, I’ve known since 2020 when I was a kid. I love to watch YouTube about Finance and technologies with business. Because my dad cooperates and opens businesses and accommodations now, I get to meet a lot of his friends and customers and I get some work to do with them such as: I help Dan from the US calculating his taxes and other transitions, which I get to meet and connect both with banks in Cambodia and in Americas. I also work with Michael (from Denmark) with the transactions from Denmark too! I am currently helping my father with his businesses too. My father does a lot of work so I have a lot of work too: Marketing, Documenting, Editing, Taxing, and more. I also attend some events outside too; I also have my YouTube channel, TikTok, and Instagram since 2020. I also participate in STEM representing Siem Reap province.

Despite all of that, doesn’t mean I’m rich yet — I just want to publish this so that my future self can forever see this when I am older 😉. My dream is to help Cambodians to get jobs (to provide a lot of jobs to Cambodians), help my nation and world, and I want to explore every part of the world and have enough money.
